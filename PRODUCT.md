# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

Installable PWA, designed phone-first. Primary test device: Samsung Galaxy S23 (Android, Chrome). It must also work on other phones and on desktop.

## Users

First the author, using it daily for their own meal planning (MVP stage). Later, people who plan meals a few days ahead and keep an eye on calories and macros.

## Product Purpose

Calendories plans meals ahead: a daily plan with calories and macros, a base of recipes and products, and a shopping list built from the plan. Success means answering two questions quickly: what do I eat in the coming days, and how much is still left for today.

## Positioning

Planning ahead instead of logging after the fact. The user composes the coming days from recipes and products, and the shopping list follows from the plan. Calorie counters log what was eaten; Calendories decides what will be eaten.

## Operating Context

- Used on a phone, often one-handed, in the kitchen, in a shop, or while planning the week.
- The profile (sex, age, height, weight, target weight, pace of change, lifestyle and training activity) drives the calorie target via Mifflin-St Jeor BMR times PAL plus a goal adjustment. Macros are calculated automatically or set manually.
- Meals of the day (names, order, visibility) are configured per user and shape the plan rows.
- Onboarding collects the same profile, goal and meal data before first use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interface language is Polish; code and identifiers are English.
- Dark theme is the default, light theme and system theme are available.
- Product data comes from Open Food Facts (ODbL) and the attribution must stay visible in the app.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Calendories (calendar plus calories), but the product is meal planning, not a calendar.
- Calm, matter-of-fact tone. No gamification.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, user counts or benchmarks exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Plan first: every screen should help decide what will be eaten, not record what was eaten.
- Show what is in the plan; show what is missing only when asked.
- Fast on a phone: few taps, thumb reach, no precision gestures for numeric data.
- One source of truth for goals: settings and onboarding edit the same profile data the same way.
